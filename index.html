✦ About the Festival ✦

What is Diwali?

Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is one of the most significant festivals in Indian culture. Celebrated across religions — Hindu, Jain, Sikh, and some Buddhist communities — it symbolises the spiritual victory of light over darkness, knowledge over ignorance, and good over evil.

During Diwali, millions of homes are lit with earthen oil lamps called diyas, colourful lanterns, and fireworks illuminate the night sky. Families exchange sweets, wear new clothes, offer prayers, and decorate their homes with intricate rangoli patterns.

This year, join us at the Community Cultural Centre, Mumbai for five days of music, dance, art, food, and lights — a true celebration of our shared heritage.
